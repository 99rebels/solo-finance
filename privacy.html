Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version

  • Solo Finance reads your Gmail inside your browser. Your emails, receipts and bank statements are never sent to us.
  • What it finds is stored in Chrome on your computer. We have no copy.
  • Gmail access is read-only. Solo Finance can't send, delete or change your email.
  • We don't sell data, show ads or use your email to train AI models.
  • If you buy a tax year, our payment provider handles the payment and we keep your email address and licence key. That's all we hold about you.

Who we are

Solo Finance ("we", "us") is a Chrome extension and this website, made by 99rebels. This policy covers both. If you have a question about it, email hello@example.com.

What Solo Finance accesses

Your Gmail

When you choose Connect Gmail, Google asks you to give Solo Finance read-only access to your mailbox (the gmail.readonly permission). Solo Finance then uses it to:

  • search for emails that look like receipts, invoices, renewal reminders, trial notices, cancellations, refunds and payment notices, leaving out Gmail's Promotions and Social tabs and mail you sent;
  • read those emails (sender, subject, date and content) and the PDF attachments of receipts and invoices, to find each purchase's supplier, date, amount, VAT and billing period;
  • check every hour for new receipts while Chrome is open, so new purchases, renewals and price changes are picked up.

All of this happens in your browser. Emails go directly from Google to Chrome on your computer and are read there.

Bank statements you import

If you import a bank statement (a CSV file you download from your bank), Solo Finance reads it in your browser to match each payment to a receipt. The file and the payments in it stay on your computer. Solo Finance never connects to your bank and never asks for your bank login.

Things you enter

Answers you give (business or personal, "still using it?"), notes, files you attach to a payment, your settings and any subscriptions you add by hand are stored the same way, on your computer.

How it's used

Only to show you your purchases and subscriptions, match them to your statement, remind you before renewals and trials, and produce the exports you ask for (the accountant pack, spreadsheets and backups). Nothing else.

Every number and sentence in Solo Finance is worked out by code running on your computer. Your emails are not sent to any AI service, ours or anyone else's.

Where it's stored

In Chrome's extension storage on your computer. To avoid downloading the same email twice, Solo Finance keeps a local copy of the emails it has read; that copy is deleted when you disconnect Gmail or delete your data. Nothing is stored on our servers, because we don't run any for your data.

Exports and backups you save are ordinary files on your computer. Where they go after that, for example to your accountant, is up to you.

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Google user data

Solo Finance's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice:

  • Gmail data is used only to provide the features described above, which you can see in the extension.
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except as needed to provide those features (for example, an export you choose to save), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale with your consent.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold and never used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads your email. We couldn't if we wanted to, because it never reaches us.

Who we share data with

We don't share your Gmail data, statements or purchase records with anyone, because we don't have them. The only data we hold, your email address and licence key if you've paid, is shared with our payment provider to process the payment and is never sold.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Disconnect Gmail (Settings): stops access and deletes the local copy of your emails.
  • Delete all data (Settings): removes everything Solo Finance has stored on your computer.
  • Uninstall the extension: Chrome deletes its storage.
  • You can also remove Solo Finance's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

We keep your email address and licence key while your licence is valid and for as long as tax law requires us to keep sales records. Ask us to delete them at any time after that.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how it's used. Email hello@example.com. For anything stored by the extension, you already have it all, on your computer.

You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie) or the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk).

Children

Solo Finance is for people running a business and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Solo Finance handles your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects Gmail data, the extension will tell you before it takes effect.
